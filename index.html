Questions

Frequently asked

Is Dynamic Market Place free to use?

Registration is free, but you need an active 12-month membership (R258.75 including VAT) to list items, publish services, or contact other members. This is a once-off payment — not a recurring subscription.

Does the platform take commission on my sales?

No. We take 0% commission. Whatever you sell an item or service for, you keep the full amount. The only cost is the annual membership fee.

How are members verified?

Every member provides their full legal name, ID or passport number, verified email address and mobile phone number during registration. Duplicate accounts are blocked automatically at the database level.

How do payments between buyers and sellers work?

All payments for goods and services are handled directly between the buyer and the seller, on whatever terms you agree. Dynamic Market Place is not a party to those transactions — we simply connect verified buyers and sellers.

Can I sell services, not just products?

Yes. Our Services module supports wellness, beauty, fitness, tutoring, home services, photography, event services and more. Service providers can set weekly availability and receive bookings directly in the app.

How long does a listing stay live?

Every listing stays active for 60 days. You'll be notified before it expires, and can renew it with one click to keep it live for another 60 days.

What is your refund policy?

If you request a refund in writing within 14 days of payment and haven't used the platform to list, publish, or contact members, we will refund in full. See our Refund Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from your Profile page in the app, or by emailing us directly. See our Privacy Policy for details.
