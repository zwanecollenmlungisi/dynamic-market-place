About Dynamic Market Place

Dynamic Market Place is a South African online marketplace built to connect verified buyers and sellers of goods and services in a safe, trusted environment.

Unlike many other platforms, every member of Dynamic Market Place is required to provide their full legal name, ID or passport number, verified email address, and mobile phone number. This identity-verification requirement dramatically reduces fraud and time-wasting, giving honest traders a platform they can trust.

Sellers can list physical products and service providers can publish bookable services — beauty, wellness, fitness, tutoring, home services, photography, events, and more. Every listing is automatically watermarked with the Dynamic Market Place badge so buyers know it's genuine.
